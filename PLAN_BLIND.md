# PLAN_BLIND — declaration beyond the security keyword, with the rule drafted blind

**Status: drafted by Claude on 2026-10-10, unsigned.** Under hard rule 2 of
`CLAUDE.md` a model may draft a band and may not sign it. **Nothing runs and no
record is written until Sergi has signed §0**, and the signature has to land
before any figure named here exists, in its own commit, staged by name. Stage B
spends. Stage C does not, and §11 says why the gate binds it anyway.

**Opened by Sergi on 2026-10-09**, on the item `STATUS.md`'s *What is open*
carries as its second: whether declaration carries direction beyond one
attribute. `PLAN_EDGES.md` and `PLAN_OVERLAP.md` both left it there:

- **Nearly every edge the proposer declared is one rule of thumb**: on a ticket
  with the security keyword, `SECURITY_INCIDENT` wins
  ([`results_edges/FINDINGS_EDGES.md`](results_edges/FINDINGS_EDGES.md),
  [`results_overlap/FINDINGS_OVERLAP.md`](results_overlap/FINDINGS_OVERLAP.md)).
- **The nine paid runs of the three protocols installed 213 edges between rules
  of different queues, and 200 of them involve a keyword rule**, a rule whose
  extension lies inside `has_security_keyword = True`. Computed while drafting,
  with the instrument of this plan; `K-g2` recomputes it.
- **Beyond the keyword, the edges declared at a birth on an impasse with a strict
  better rule number 0, 3 and 3** under v1, v1e and v2e, three runs each.

**Why more draws or another corpus would not supply them.** The proposer
partitions. Read beyond the keyword, nearly every rule it writes meets no earlier
rule of another queue there: `K-a`'s statistic reads 1.0000, 0.9667 and 1.0000 on
the median of the three protocols' runs. Rung 2 measured why
([`results2/FINDINGS2.md`](results2/FINDINGS2.md), *What is verifiable*, §1):
**seeing the base is what cuts the overlap.** Without the base in view the same
model's rules overlapped on 17.5% of their pairs at n=100, and with it on 1.6%,
at the same density of conditions. It does not narrow its rules; it picks
attribute combinations that tile the space. The same tickets arrive first in every
draw of the canonical corpus, and a corpus without the keyword would remove the
one rule that crosses the tiling and leave the tiling.

**So this plan takes the base out of the first call, and keeps the declaration.**
On an impasse the proposer drafts its rule with no rule shown, as rung 1 did. The
engine then lists the existing rules of another queue the draft overlaps, and one
placement round asks for the same rule, placed against each one, as v2e's
discipline does. A rule written blind cannot dodge the base, and a rule placed
afterwards has to declare where it crosses.

**What it would change.**

- **If `K-b` holds**, declaration at write time carries direction beyond the
  keyword, on rules the proposer did not tile.
- **If `K-c` holds too**, it carries something a fixed ranking of the queues does
  not: the first evidence that the second way supplies priority beyond a rule of
  thumb, and the second clause of `STATUS.md`'s headline is revisited.
- **If `K-b` holds and `K-c` is refuted**, the pairwise thread's finding holds at
  write time too: direction, and none of it beyond a ranking.
- **If `K-b` is refuted**, beyond the keyword the declarations point at the
  better rule no more than six times in ten, and the keyword was most of what the
  channel carried.

**Either way the precondition is a row of its own**, `K-a`, so that the rows on
direction cannot go without a verdict for a reason no row names.

## 2. The choices that have to be made at signature time

None of them moves a band. **Signing §0 adopts the drafter's choice in each
unless the signature line says otherwise.**

- **The blind draft on an impasse only.** A CONFLICT shows the rules in conflict
  and goes through v2e's path, because an order answer has to cite them. The
  drafter recommends **yes**. Under a forced placement a CONFLICT needs a refused
  declaration to arise at all, and v2e met one in three runs.
- **The same rule in the placement answer.** A placement answer whose rule sends
  the ticket to another queue or covers other cases is refused, `cambiada`. The
  drafter recommends **yes**: a rule that may change once it sees what it
  overlaps may narrow itself out of the overlap, which is what this plan exists to
  stop. The alternative, a changed rule validated afresh, is the partition's way
  back in.
- **The exemption: overlaps with a rule of the same queue owe no declaration.**
  v2e's. The drafter recommends **yes**.
- **Nested pairs of different queues: a declaration still required.** v2e's. The
  drafter recommends **yes**.
- **Placement rounds, `K`: 1.** As v2e's repair round. A rule left unplaced is
  refused and counted.
- **The order answer on a CONFLICT: admitted**, v2e's, unchanged.
- **`R`, the number of runs: 3.** As every baseline.
- **The ranking K-c reads against: rung 3's Stage C hierarchy.** The drafter
  recommends **yes**: it is the pairwise thread's control, fixed before any run of
  this plan and fitted on another object. The alternatives are the best of the
  40,320 rankings, chosen with the labels, which is a ceiling and not a control,
  and their mean, which is a level for a ranking picked blind.
- **The unit: the older rule.** The alternatives are the declared winner and the
  birth. The paragraph under §0's table says why.
- **A control arm.** The drafter recommends **none**: the three baselines are the
  controls, and the blind draft against v2e is the one change the rows read.
- **`PREDICTION.md`.** This plan drafts no entry there and its gate does not look
  for one.

## 5. The protocol, v2b: v2e with a blind draft and a forced placement

**Everything not named here is v2e as it produced `PLAN_OVERLAP.md`'s records.**
From v2e: the system prompt, the exemption, the validator, the citations, the
copy refusal, the order answer, the installation, and on a CONFLICT the screen and
the repair round. The model's settings are every baseline's. The draft wording
below is the drafter's. The package freezes it by a fingerprint, and Sergi may
change it before signing and not after.

**5.1 — The blind draft.** v2e's system prompt gains one paragraph, before v2's
paragraph on set arithmetic, and loses nothing:

> *CUANDO NINGUNA REGLA CUBRE EL TICKET, escribes la regla SIN VER la base: no se
> te muestra ninguna regla existente. Escribela al nivel de abstraccion que creas
> correcto para el ticket, con `beats` y `loses_to` vacios. Despues el motor
> calcula, sobre el espacio completo de casos, con que reglas existentes de OTRA
> cola se solapa la tuya, y te las lista: entonces tienes que situar ESA MISMA
> regla frente a cada una, sin cambiarla. Cuando el ticket escala porque varias
> reglas existentes chocan, esas reglas si se te muestran.*

On an impasse the first call shows, where v2e rendered the neighbourhood:

> *BASE DE REGLAS: no se te muestra en este paso. Escribe tu regla para este
> ticket; si se solapa con reglas existentes de OTRA cola, se te listaran
> despues.*

**5.2 — The placement round.** The draft is validated as v2e validates a rule: an
order answer on an impasse is refused, the rule must match the ticket, and a copy
is refused. `O` is v2e's: the existing rules whose extension meets the draft's and
whose queue differs. `S`, those of its own queue, is recorded and owes nothing.
**With `O` empty the draft is born, and nothing is declared.** Otherwise one
placement round lists `O` in v2e's seeded order, recorded with the call, under
this message:

> *Tu regla se solapa con estas reglas existentes de OTRA cola (calculado por el
> motor sobre el espacio completo de casos): [lista]. Responde de nuevo con el
> mismo formato y con LA MISMA regla: la misma accion y las mismas condiciones.
> Situala frente a cada una de la lista: ponla en `beats` si tu regla es una
> EXCEPCION a ella, de modo que la tuya manda donde casen las dos; en `loses_to`
> si es un caso GENERAL por debajo de ella, de modo que la otra manda. No cambies
> la regla: una regla distinta se rechaza.*

The second answer must be a rule, must match the ticket, **must be the draft's
rule**, the same queue and the same extension whatever its wording, and must
place every rule of `O`. Each failure has its verdict, and a refused rule is not
installed: an order answer, `orden_sin_conflicto`; an invalid rule, `rechazada`;
another rule, **`cambiada`**, the one verdict v2b adds; a rule of `O` unplaced, or
no answer at all, `sin_situar`. Citations may name only the rules listed.
Installation goes through `try_edge`, and its verdict is recorded. The case keeps
the proposer's last answer as its decision, as v2e's does.

**5.3 — A CONFLICT is v2e's.** Its screen, its order paragraph, its repair
round, word for word, through `overlap.loop.propose_v2e`, with every installed
edge tagged by its channel.

**5.4 — What every call records**: v2e's fields, `S` beside every `O`, and the
list of a placement round.

## 6. Known traps

**6.1 — The protocol is a bundle.** The blind draft, the placement round and the
same-rule requirement change together against v2e. The rows read the bundle, and
§13 says what that leaves unseparated.

**6.2 — Drafting blind changes the rules, not only their overlap.** A rule
written without the base may repeat, cover or contradict what is there. The copy
refusal still applies, and K-f reports the rules and verdicts. `FINDINGS_FIDELITY.md`
found a sign that a screen of compiled rules costs the model accuracy; a first
call without one may move the proposer's action accuracy the other way, and K-f
reports it beside every baseline's.

**6.3 — A changed rule is refused.** If the proposer disagrees with its draft
once it sees what it overlaps, the disagreement is a refusal, `cambiada`, and is
counted. The rows do not read refused rules.

**6.4 — The lists grow.** Late in a run a placement round can list many rules.
In v2e's run 2 a rule that overlapped 34 was left unplaced. `K = 1`, and every
`sin_situar` is counted with the size of its list.

**6.5 — The listing order.** `PLAN_PRIMACY.md` found that the slot decides at
least about one pairwise answer in twelve. A placement list is shuffled with a
fixed seed and its order recorded, so a slot effect can be read afterwards. It
adjudicates nothing.

**6.6 — The ranking reads no rule, and it is not ignorant.** Stage C's hierarchy
was fitted on the hidden policy's pairs. It is the pairwise thread's control,
kept so that K-c reads what that thread read. A row that beat it would beat a
ranking that already encodes much of the policy's layer order.

**6.7 — The unit weighs a rule once.** A rule many births declare against counts
as one, and a rule declared against once counts as much. The pooled reading is
reported beside it.

**6.8 — The half without the keyword is a surface, and not the corpus.** K-b and
K-d read it uniformly, K-e reads the arrivals. Every figure names which.

**6.9 — Read whole, not as it grew.** The rows read each final base from case 0.
What the loop decided while the base grew is K-f's.

**6.10 — Level 1 is not overridable here.** A declaration that a narrower rule
loses to a rule containing it is refused as `contradice_subsuncion` and recorded.

**6.11 — The rarest queue.** The escalation trigger is v1's, and `U-d` found it
never asks about `ONCALL_ESCALATION`. K-f counts it again and expects zero.

**6.12 — The date.** v1 was asked on 2026-09-30, v1e on 2026-10-05 and v2e on
2026-10-06 and 07. Every comparison names the dates.

## 13. What this plan cannot settle, declared before it runs

1. **The blind draft apart from the placement.** They come together: a blind draft
   without a placement would be rung 1's protocol on rung 2's engine, and a
   placement without a blind draft is v2e.
2. **The rules apart from the channel.** Blind rules are not v2e's rules. If the
   direction differs from the baselines', the record cannot say whether the
   declarations or the rules they are about made the difference.
3. **The material.** No declaration changes which queue the model writes, and
   `T3_ENGINEERING` and `ACCOUNT_MANAGER` keep their missing rules (item (d) of
   `CHAT_SUMMARY.md`'s erratum to §4).
4. **One ranking.** K-c reads one fixed ranking. A row that beat it would not have
   beaten every ranking, and the best of them, chosen with the labels, is a
   ceiling no control reaches.
5. **A trigger that would ask about the rarest queue.** v1's trigger is kept, and
   none is specified.
6. **Whether level 1 should yield to a declaration.** K-f counts every refused
   one. It does not ask what overriding subsumption would buy.
7. **One model, one corpus, `R` draws.** `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash`, as every
   paid record.
