**The item.** The proposer has always been told where it erred, never what the
right queue was. At level 3 it did not need to be told. At level 1 it does, and
no run has told it.

- **Told where, never what.** `IDEAS.md`'s adjudication of the review says so,
  under *What the adjudication adds*.

  - Since then it has had level 3's 29 rules, whose first match is the policy at

    unmeasured.
- **At level 3 a label would add almost nothing.** In the six v1c runs of
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` and `PLAN_WHOLE.md`, the first call at a reported error

    16 escalations of `ACCOUNT_MANAGER`.
  - **All of that is with reasoning off.** No record holds level 1 with reasoning
    on, in either shape.
- **The label is what could teach the remainder.** At level 1 the report's *what*
  is information the prompt does not hold. `IDEAS.md` calls this *the first
  protocol in which the system could learn what its prompt does not hold*.

**The question, in its cleanest form.** With level 1's catalogue in the prompt,
reasoning on, `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at coverage 1/2 and v1c's placement
round:

- does a report that carries the ticket's true queue teach the loop what the
  catalogue does not hold?
- does it teach the two queues the catalogue misses?
- does compiling the corrections one ticket at a time beat inducing a base from
  the same corrections at once?
- does the loop beat learners handed the same labels and not the catalogue?
- does it compile the rarest queue where the channel supplies it?

**Two arms.** Both run `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s v1c at level 1: `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s
channel arm with v1c's placement round. That is `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`'s loop on
rung 2's engine, at n=2000 and seed 17, with reasoning on.

- **The trigger.** A case escalates on an IMPASSE or a CONFLICT, and on a wrong
  decision whose draw falls below 1/2.
- **The runs.** Each arm has three runs, and the arms alternate. Run `k` of each
  arm reads the draws of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s c r`k`, and every call goes to one
  provider.
- **N, without the label**, is v1c with level 1's system prompt,
  `manual.prompts.loop_system_prompt(1)`, in place of level 3's. Every other text
  is v1c's, byte for byte: v2b's `BLIND_BASE`, the placement message and the retry
  message. A reported error escalates as in every channel run. The proposer is
  handed the ticket, and is not told that the base erred.
- **L, with the label**, is N with one change, made only at a reported error.
  - The first call's user message carries a report paragraph after `BLIND_BASE`
    (§2.1). It says the engine decided the ticket, that a reviewer marked the
    decision wrong, and what the ticket's true queue is.
  - The placement call repeats the conversation, so it carries the paragraph
    too.
  - Impasses and conflicts are asked as in N.

**Set beside L's runs, on L's own labels.**

- **At once.** Under level 1's compile prompt, with reasoning on, the model is
  handed the labelled tickets of an L run. It is asked for the whole base in one
  answer, at checkpoints along the run and at its end (§2.4). Five asks with no
  label give what the catalogue alone compiles to with reasoning on.
- **The learners**, trained on the same labelled tickets, free:
  - `PLAN_ILP.md`'s inducer, at both its beams;
  - `PLAN_STUDENTS.md`'s pruned tree, logistic regression and cache.
- **The table of tickets** is the floor: a labelled ticket decides only itself.
  257 of the 2,000 arrivals repeat an earlier ticket (`results/FINDINGS.md`
  counts 1,743 unique).
- **The control on the proposer's own answers**, which `CLAUDE.md` asks of any
  baseline set against the proposer. The students are trained on every one of
  L's escalations, with the label where reported and the proposer's queue
  elsewhere. Reported.

**What it changes in what is measured, said first.**

- **The arms differ in one paragraph, at reported errors**, and in nothing else
  the proposer is handed. The paragraph carries two things: that the ticket was
  decided and reported, and its true queue. No row tells them apart. A third arm
  would (§2.2, §13).
- **The labelled tickets are L's own errors.** They lean toward where its base
  erred, and they depend on that base.
  - Every learner and every at-once ask sees exactly those tickets, and no other

  - They are matched to L on labels. They are not matched on what L's proposer
    drafted at impasses, which carries no truth.
- **The learners are not handed the catalogue, and the at-once asks are.** A row
  that sets the loop against a learner reads the catalogue with it, and says so.
- **The surfaces.**
  - **The space is the out-of-sample surface.** It holds the labelled tickets as
    points: at most as many as a run's escalations, under three in a thousand of
    its 134,400.
  - **The arrivals are in-sample** wherever a label was read. So the corpus
    figure is split into the tickets labelled and the rest. The online figure,
    each decision as it was taken, is given beside them, as `IDEAS.md` asks of
    the next §0.
- **It breaks the oracle's separation on purpose, through the trigger**, as
  `PLAN_MANUAL.md` broke it through its texts.
  - **Where the label comes from.** It comes from the corpus labels that
    `PLAN_REUSE.md`'s records hold, as every trigger since `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`
    reads them, and never from the oracle.
  - **What watches it.** `tests/test_oracle_separation.py` watches the package's
    proposer-path modules. A test of the package pins that a label reaches a
    prompt only at L's reported escalations, and only that ticket's own label.
- **At level 1 the proposer has no manual identifiers to cite.**
  - The loop's system prompt says `beats` and `loses_to` may cite only rules of
    the list above.
  - At level 3 that list was the 29 rules. The loop's proposer cited them in
    place of the base's own, and the engine dropped every such declaration

    citation in six (`results_reasoned_loop/FINDINGS_REASONED_LOOP.md`).
  - At level 1 only the placement round's rules carry identifiers.
- **Reasoning is on**, at high effort, for the loop and for the at-once asks
  alike. It has been on in every loop since `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`, against

- **Every row reads the space, within one period and one provider.** Every

## 0. Predictions — bands and refutation lines

Drafted, unsigned. One row is one event. **A band's edge is its own refutation
line**, so band and refutation partition the axis and nothing can fall between
them.

**The runs.** `R = 3` runs per arm.
- **The loop.** Each run is `PLAN_REUSE.md`'s loop over the 2,000 cases of
  `generate_corpus(2000, seed=17)`, with a fresh rung 2 engine, under
  `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at coverage 1/2.
- **The draws.** Run `k` of either arm reads the draws of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s
  `c r{k}`.
- **The order.** The arms alternate, as §2.3 sets.

**The statistics.** Each is a ratio of integer counts, or a difference of such
ratios. **Each is compared with its line exactly, as a fraction.** No rounding
enters a verdict.

- **`B`, `space(B)` and `recall_q(B)`** are `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s, computed by
  the same functions. `B` is a run's final base, rebuilt from its record. A point
  the base leaves undecided counts as wrong.
- **`labels(r)`.** The reported errors of run `r`, in arrival order, each a
  ticket and its true queue. In L they reach the proposer. In N they are counted
  and reach nothing.
- **`followed(r)`.** Take L run `r`'s first calls at reported tickets whose
  answer names one of the eight queues. `followed(r)` is the share of them that
  name the ticket's true queue.
- **`pair(B)`.** The mean of `recall_ACCOUNT_MANAGER(B)` and
  `recall_T3_ENGINEERING(B)` over the space. That is `G-b`'s statistic.
- **`A(r)`.** The at-once asks from all of `labels(r)`. `space(A(r))` is the
  median of their `space`, each read as `PLAN_MANUAL.md`'s compile asks were
  read (§5.4).
- **`best(r)`.** The largest `space` among the learners trained on `labels(r)`:
  the inducer at beam 40 and at beam 120, the tree, the logistic regression and
  the cache. Each is read as its own record reads it (§5.5).
- **The median** of three runs is the middle one, and of two runs their mean.

**The unit, *thin*, and when a row has no value.** All of it is settled here and
not in the scorer.

- **Every adjudicated row reads medians.** Either it reads medians over an arm's
  runs, or the median over L's runs of a difference taken run by run. With three
  runs an arm a sign test cannot reach 1/20, whatever the effect's size. So every
  row is a band reading, as every loop plan since `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` read its
  rows.
- **A verdict on a median is labelled *thin*** when any one run of an arm the row
  reads, put in place of that arm's median, would put the statistic on the other
  side of its line. That is `PLAN_MANUAL.md`'s test, as `PLAN_WHOLE.md` applied
  it. The label does not change the verdict.
- **What one run does to `CR-f`.** The draws of run 2 make no ticket of `H05`
  reportable, and `H05`'s part is a third of the queue's points: 2,100 of 6,300
  (`results_channel/FINDINGS_CHANNEL.md`). Under those draws the transcriber
  recalls 2/3 of the queue. A rule written at a ticket of `H04` might reach
  `H05`'s region too. Unless one does, `CR-f` holds only if runs 1 and 3 both
  reach its line.
- **A run is complete** when it reaches case 2,000, or the cap of §1.
  - The drafter expects N's runs to come near the cap (§1). A run at the cap is
    complete, and its base is read as it stands.
  - A run that cannot be completed leaves its arm with fewer runs. The rows read
    the runs that exist, and the record names the missing one.
  - A row that reads an arm with fewer than two runs has no value.
  - **`CR-a` has no value for a run with no first call at a reported ticket that
    names a queue.**
  - **`CR-d` has no value for a run without a valid at-once ask** from all of its
    labels, after the re-asks §2.4 allows.
- **A row with no value is unadjudicable**, and the record says so.

## 2. The choices that have to be made at signature time

### 2.1 The report's text — Sergi's choice; the drafter recommends R1

Both candidates stand after `BLIND_BASE` in L's first call, and only at a reported
error. They answer the first call's framing, which calls every escalation an
impasse. Neither names a rule.

- **R1, the queue alone.**

  > AVISO DE REVISION: este ticket no es un impasse. El motor lo decidio, y un
  > revisor ha marcado esa decision como erronea. La cola correcta de este
  > ticket es {queue}.

- **R2, the queue and the engine's decision.**

  > AVISO DE REVISION: este ticket no es un impasse. El motor lo envio a
  > {engine_action}, y un revisor ha marcado esa decision como erronea. La cola
  > correcta de este ticket es {queue}.

**Why the drafter recommends R1.** R1 adds the label and the fact of the report,
and nothing the at-once asks and the learners are not also handed. R2 adds the
engine's decision. Any deployment knows that decision, but no competitor here is
handed it. `IDEAS.md` keeps it apart, under *What `PLAN_WHOLE.md` opens*, *The
frame*.

### 2.2 Two arms, not three — the drafter recommends it

A third arm, R1 without its last sentence, would tell the proposer that the base
erred and not what the right queue is. It would separate the label from the
report. It is likely to escalate as much as N and so to cost as much again, about
two more days. The drafter recommends leaving it to the next plan, and §13
declares what that leaves open.

### 2.3 Three runs an arm, the arms alternating, and the cap — the drafter recommends it

- **The runs.** Three runs of each arm, alternating, in the order a fixed seed
  draws for the first arm.
- **The draws.** Run `k` of both arms reads `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s draws of
  `c r{k}`.
- **The cap** is 400 escalations a run.

All three follow `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`.

**What six pairs would cost.** Each draw run twice an arm gives the six
alternated pairs `IDEAS.md` asks for, on which a sign test can reach 1/20. It
also measures what nondeterminism alone does to a base, which `IDEAS.md` keeps
as *The same draws twice in one period*. It doubles the time.

**A lower cap** would shorten N's runs. It would also bind L's, if L needs more
labels than the drafter guesses.

### 2.4 The at-once asks — the drafter recommends this design

- **The prompt.**
  - **The system prompt** is `manual.prompts.compile_system_prompt(1)`.
  - **The user message** lists the labelled tickets, in arrival order, and ends
    with `PLAN_MANUAL.md`'s compile request, byte for byte:

    > CORRECCIONES DE REVISORES: el sistema decidio mal cada uno de estos
    > tickets, y un revisor le dio su cola correcta.
    > {each ticket, as the loop shows it, with "cola_correcta"}
    >
    > Escribe ahora la base de reglas completa.

- **The checkpoints** fall at 10, 20, 40, 80, 160 and 320 labels where a run
  reaches them, and at all of its labels. Each checkpoint gets three asks.
- **With no label**, five asks of `PLAN_MANUAL.md`'s request at level 1 are made
  right after the smoke run, with reasoning on.
- **When they are asked.** Right after each L run, before the next run, through
  `harness/ask_pool.py` at up to four workers. It is asks only, never a loop.
  - As that module asks of any plan that uses it, the record keeps the workers.
  - The seconds are measured at that concurrency.
  - The smoke ask measures the pace and the refusals for the rate. A refusal
    falls back to one ask at a time.
  - The breaker counts failures in the order the answers come back.
- **An ask with no valid base** is asked again, up to twice. The validity flag is
  §0's and is tested on an empty object before signature, as `PLAN_HALVES.md`'s
  lesson asks.
- **The settings.** Reasoning is on at high effort. The budget is measured by the
  smoke ask.

### 2.5 The learners — the drafter recommends them as signed

- **The inducer** is `PLAN_ILP.md`'s, at its two beams, 40 and 120.
- **The students** are `PLAN_STUDENTS.md`'s three, as that plan signed them.
- **The table** is the floor.
- **The students of L's own answers** are the control.

All of them are trained free in Stage C, at every checkpoint, after their own
gates pass.

### 2.6 An empty object from the provider is kept as v1c records it — the drafter recommends it

The runs pinned to DeepInfra got an empty object on dozens of calls. An empty
object parses, so the retry by cause does not ask again. The loop records it as a
rule refused as invalid (`IDEAS.md`, *What the adjudication adds*).
- **The recommendation.** Changing that would change v1c. So it is kept, counted
  by arm and cause, and reported.
- **What it costs L.** Every labelled ticket an empty object lost is named. The
  next report in its region brings the label again.

### 2.7 The smoke run is 30 cases an arm on the smoke draws — the drafter recommends it

It reads the first 30 of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s smoke draws: N, then L. Then come
one at-once ask on L's smoke labels and one with no label.
- **What it checks:** the pin, the texts, the label's route and the records.
- **What it measures:** the price and the pace.
- No row reads its answers.

### 2.8 Where the runs are launched — Sergi's choice; the drafter has no recommendation

The runs can be launched in Sergi's terminal. Or the agent can launch them,
through a supervisor detached from its harness, one run at a time, as
`PLAN_WHOLE.md` §2.5 allowed. A period of three to four days should expect WSL to
stop overnight, as it did under `PLAN_HALVES.md`'s second pass, and a run to
resume from its partial file.

### 2.9 The pin is DeepInfra's fp8 endpoint, with no bridge — by the rule agreed on 2026-11-06

Every arm runs inside the plan, so every row is read within one provider.
`PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s and `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s pin met no refusal for the rate, and
billed one price throughout.

### 2.10 Everything else is `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s v1c, as signed

The model, the temperature, the reasoning setting and the budget; the retries by
cause and the waits; the resume and each call's own seconds; the records; the

## 6. Known traps

- **The label could reach a prompt by another route.** §4 fixes the one route,
  and a test pins it. `tests/test_oracle_separation.py` watches the package's
  proposer-path modules as the authority channel. This plan breaks
  `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s rule B on purpose, and says so here.
- **"Level 1" means three things in this tree.** See the preamble.
- **The framing still says impasse.** The system prompt's *"El motor simbolico no
  ha podido decidir este ticket"* and the heading *TICKET EN IMPASSE* stay
  byte for byte. The report paragraph says the ticket is not an impasse. How the
  model reads the two together is part of what L measures.
- **N may spin.** In N, the proposer's draft at a reported ticket may be the same
  wrong queue its base holds. Then the base does not change, and the next report
  in that region escalates again. That is how N comes near the cap.
- **The labelled tickets are a skewed sample.** They are L's errors, and every
  learner trained on them inherits the skew.
- **Regions no reportable ticket reaches.** No ticket governed by `H21`, `H25` or
  `H27` is reportable under these draws. A base gets those regions from the
  catalogue, or not at all.
- **A learner always answers.** The cache and the tree decide every point, and
  the loop's base may leave points undecided. `space` counts an undecided point
  as wrong for every one of them.
- **The at-once answer is a list.** Its order is read by §5.4. A list that puts a
  narrow rule below a broad one of another queue loses the narrow rule's points,
  in both readings.
- **One ticket's story can mislead.** Check it against the run's other tickets of
  its region before citing it.
- **A median of three runs moves on one run.** *Thin* says so, as §0 sets.

*To be completed against the package.*

## 13. What this plan cannot settle, declared before it runs

1. **Whether the label or the report carries a gain.** L's paragraph says both.
   The third arm of §2.2 would separate them.
2. **What no arrival exercises.** No label reaches a region without a reportable
   ticket (`ARBITRATION_REPORT.md` §9.2's erratum). The space keeps that part of
   the gap by construction.
3. **Whether the signal dies out as the base improves.** `CHAT_SUMMARY.md` §1
   measured that for an order. `CR-g` reports the reports by decile, and bands
   nothing on them.
4. **A reviewer who errs, reports late, or reports less.** Noise, delay and lower
   coverage are other plans. So is a channel with no truth at all.
5. **Drift under a manual that lags.** This regime is what it reduces to
   (`IDEAS.md`), but no drift is run.
6. **`H14`'s threshold under the prose**, with a few labelled tickets at its
   boundary. It is ask-only, at level 2, and another plan.
7. **Why the rarest queue goes either way at level 1.** `CR-f` reads it, and the
   reason is POST-RUN.
8. **One model, one provider, one manual level, one coverage, one arrival order,
   and three runs an arm.**
