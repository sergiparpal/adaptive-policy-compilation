**The item.** Told that no rule of the base matches its ticket, the loop's first
call writes the ticket's governing region about as often as told that the base
is empty. `PLAN_CAUSE.md` measured it asked alone, and no loop has sent it.

- **What `PLAN_CAUSE.md` measured.** In one period, on DeepInfra, at the 136
  tickets the reasoning loops had escalated, with level 3's manual and reasoning
  on:

    its right-queue answers, beside the empty base's 0.7491 and v2b's
    paragraph's 0.3295 (`CA-c` holds);
  - its reasoning stopped naming a conflict between two listed rules: 0.0607 of
    its right-queue answers did, against 0.7424 under v2b's paragraph (`CA-f`

    wrote the region in 0.7212, and v2b's paragraph in 0.3962 (`CA-g`).
- **What its readings found** (`FINDINGS_CAUSE.md`, *Two things Stage C left
  free*, B, and *One thing the two readings left free*, C).
  - **The notice is read as the governing rule missing from the base**, and the
    model writes the rule back.
  - **But not at a default.** Where the governing rule's region holds four or
    more rules of another queue, the model mostly writes instead a rule nested

    notice wrote 5 of 26 right-queue answers whole at those rules' tickets, 25

- **What no row has read: what a loop does with it.** At an impasse the loop
  knows that no rule matched. It is the engine's own outcome, and reads no

  Whether whole first calls at the impasses compile more of the policy, and what
  the placement round then does with them, is `IDEAS.md`'s question.

**None of this is new analysis, and the plan's part is the instrument.** The
readings are the analysis, and they are POST-RUN. What this plan adds is the
notice sent where it is true, inside the loop, beside the same loop without it,
in one period, of one provider, with bands signed before any of its figures

**What the drafter found before drafting, and why this design.** All of it is
free, and none of it reads an answer this plan buys. It reads
`PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s and `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s v1c runs, six in two periods, and
`PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s three v1d runs. §0 gives the figures, numbered, with their

- **An impasse is about two escalations in five under v1c**: 197 of 497, pooled
  over the six runs, from 26 of 88 to 35 of 72 by run (figure 1).
- **At its impasses v1c's first call writes the part whole about one time in
  three**: 66 of its 184 right-queue drafts, pooled, from 10 of 36 to 13 of 27
  by run (figure 2). v1d, whose first call asked for the region whole at every
  escalation, wrote it at its impasses in 30 of 46.
- **At the defaults it is rarer still**: 3 of 44 under v1c, where the governing
  rule's region holds four or more rules of another queue (figure 3). About a
  quarter of v1c's right-queue drafts at its impasses fall there.
- **The round mostly lets a whole draft at an impasse be born**: 56 of v1c's 66,
  and 24 of v1d's 30 (figure 4).
- **What v1c's bases lose lies mostly where no impasse reaches** (figure 5). In
  five runs of six, more than nine tenths of the points a final base does not
  decide right lie under listed rules whose tickets met no impasse in the run:
  mostly the rarest queue's, the security keyword's and severity 1's for
  business accounts, escalated at reported errors and a few conflicts.
- **The sixth run is the exception, and it turns on one ticket.** In
  `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s v1c r3, 8,400 of the 10,450 points lost lie under `H01`.
  `H01`'s first ticket, case 44, met an impasse in three runs of six, and v1c's
  first call cut its region every time (figure 6). Two of those runs wrote the
  region whole later, at another ticket. v1c r3 never did.

So the notice acts at about two escalations in five, on drafts the round mostly
lets be born, and the points v1c's bases lose lie mostly elsewhere. The design
reads the notice where it acts and the base where the loss is, and bands both.

**The question, in its cleanest form.** With level 3's manual in the prompt,
reasoning on, the channel at coverage 1/2 and v1c's placement round:

- told at an impasse that no rule of the base matches, does the loop's first
  call write the governing region whole, as it did asked alone?
- does it still narrow at the defaults?
- does the round let the whole drafts be born?
- do they leave fewer impasses after them?
- does the base then decide more of the function, or less?

**Two arms.** Both run `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s v1c: `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel arm
with v1c's placement round. That is `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`'s loop on rung 2's
engine, at n=2000 and seed 17, with level 3's manual and reasoning on. A case
escalates on an IMPASSE or a CONFLICT, and on a wrong decision whose draw falls
below 1/2. Each arm has three runs, the arms alternate, and every call goes to
one provider.

- **v1c** is `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s arm, called and not changed. This is the
  third period it runs in, after that plan's and `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s.
- **v1n** is v1c with one change, at the first call of an escalation whose cause
  is an IMPASSE (§5.2). There, v2b's `BLIND_BASE`, which stands where the base
  would be, is followed by a blank line and the impasse notice: `PLAN_CAUSE.md`'s
  arm `i`'s paragraph, byte for byte. At a CONFLICT and at a reported ERROR, v1n's
  first call is v1c's, byte for byte. Every other text is v1c's: the system
  prompt, the placement message and the retry message.

**What it changes in what is measured, said first.**

- **The arms differ in one paragraph, at one cause.** At an impasse, v1n's first
  user message carries the notice, and the placement call, which repeats the
  conversation, carries it too. At every other escalation the two arms send the
  same bytes. The bases part from the first impasse onward, so the tickets each
  arm escalates, and their causes, differ.
- **The notice reads no label.** An impasse is the engine's own outcome: no rule
  of the base matches the ticket. The loop hands v1n's proposer whether the
  escalation is an impasse, and nothing else. At a reported error it hands it
  what it hands it at a conflict: nothing. Neither the label, nor the draw, nor
  that the base erred reaches the proposer, in either arm.
- **The notice is true wherever it is sent.** At an impasse no rule of the base
  matches the ticket, and that is why the engine could not decide it. The system
  prompt's frame, *"El motor simbolico no ha podido decidir este ticket"*, is
  true there too. At a reported error the frame stays false in both arms, as in
  every loop since `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md` (§6.1).
- **A whole draft at an impasse takes later impasses away.** Each region written
  whole is a region later tickets no longer reach as impasses. So the notice
  limits its own reach: the more it is followed, the fewer first calls carry it.
  `IM-e` reads that, and `IM-a`'s denominator falls with it.
- **At the defaults the notice is expected to narrow**, as `FINDINGS_CAUSE.md`'s
  readings found it asked alone. A rule nested in a missing default decides its
  own combination and leaves the rest of the default's region to later
  impasses. `IM-b` bands it, as `IDEAS.md` asks.
- **The notice cannot reach the rarest queue's tickets directly.** In the 24
  reasoning loop runs `PLAN_CAUSE.md` §0 counted, every escalation at
  `ONCALL_ESCALATION`'s seven tickets was a reported error or a conflict, and
  none an impasse: by the time they arrive, a broader rule decides them. What
  the notice changes there it changes through the base. No row bands the
  queue, and `IM-g` reports it.
- **Whole is breadth, not correctness.** *"Narrow ≠ correct"*
  (`ARBITRATION_REPORT.md` §2), and whole is not right either. A whole draft of
  the right queue can still be placed on the wrong side, and a whole draft of a
  default holds rules of other queues that must beat it. `IM-c` and `IM-d` read
  what the base decides.
- **A share can rise by construction.** `whole_I` counts a draft wider than its
  part as whole, and leaves out of its denominator the drafts of another queue
  and those naming no valid rule. An arm can raise its share by drafting wider,
  or by losing the right queue where it would have cut. `IM-g` reports both by
  arm, and every draft's breadth.
- **What the round keeps refusing** is v1c's: a crossing rule, or one equal to
  the draft, whose conflict is still open, left without a side. A whole draft
  meets longer lists than a cut does, and `IM-f` reads what the round does with
  the whole drafts.
- **The order comes from v1c's round, as in `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`.** Containment
  orders nested rules, and the round asks for a side against the rules it lists.
  A citation of the manual's identifiers, `H01` to `H29`, is dropped, because no
  base holds them, in both arms.
- **The pairing fixes which cases can be reported, not which are.** Run `k` of
  both arms reads one set of draws. The bases differ between the arms, and so do
  their wrong decisions.
- **Both arms break rung 2's invariant**, as `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s,
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s and `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s did. The trigger reads the truth of
  every arrival, and acts on half the wrong decisions.
- **Reasoning is on**, as in every loop since `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`, against
  `PLAN_REUSE.md` §1's amendment, for this plan's calls only.
- **Hard rule 5 allows the notice.** It is a recorded text: `PLAN_CAUSE.md` asked
  it at every one of `PLAN_HALVES.md`'s 136 tickets, on both passes, and its
  rows were adjudicated on those answers. No text here is new.
- **Every row is read within one provider and one period.** Every comparison with
  another plan's runs crosses dates, and is reported in `IM-g`.

**The runs.** `R = 3` runs per arm. Each run is `PLAN_REUSE.md`'s loop over the
2,000 cases of `generate_corpus(2000, seed=17)`, with a fresh rung 2 engine. Its
trigger is `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at coverage 1/2. Run `k` of either arm
reads the draws of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s `c r{k}`, as `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s and
`PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s did. The arms alternate, as §2.3 sets.

**The statistics.** Each is a ratio of integer counts. **Each is compared with its
line exactly, as a fraction.** No rounding enters a verdict.

- **`B`, `space(B)`, `recall_q(B)`, `calls(r)` and `refused(r)`** are
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s, computed by the same functions, called.
  - **`B`** is a run's final base, rebuilt from the record and held to the
    engine the run ended with.
  - **`space(B)`** is the share of the 134,400 points that `B` decides right
    under rung 2's engine. A CONFLICT or an IMPASSE is not right.
  - **`recall_q(B)`** is the share of the points whose true queue is `q` that
    `B` sends to `q`.
  - **`refused(r)`** is the share of run `r`'s escalations that ended *unplaced*
    with an answer.
- **An escalation's cause** is the one its record keeps, `channel.loop.escalates`'s:
  IMPASSE, CONFLICT or ERROR.
- **`impasses(r)`** is the number of run `r`'s escalations whose cause is an
  IMPASSE.
- **A first-call draft** is the first call's answer at an escalation, born or
  not, read by `placement/cost.py`'s `draft_class`, called. Its class is
  *whole*, *cut by one* listed rule, *cut by two*, *other*, *wrong queue* or *no
  rule*.
  - **Its part** is the points of its ticket's governing rule's region whose
    true queue is that rule's. The governing rule is the first listed rule whose
    region holds the ticket.
  - **It is *whole*** when it names the governing rule's queue and every point of
    its part lies in its extension. A draft wider than the part is whole too.
  - **An empty object parses.** The asker does not ask again, the loop records
    the rule refused as invalid, and the draft is *no rule* (*The retry*,
    below).
  - **A first call that never came back has no draft.** It is counted and
    reported.
- **`whole_I(r)`.** Of run `r`'s first-call drafts at its impasses that name the
  right queue and a valid rule, the share classed *whole*. Drafts of another
  queue, and drafts naming no valid rule, are left out of the share, above and
  below. They are counted and reported.
- **The defaults, `D`**, are the four listed rules whose regions hold four or more
  rules of another queue: `H08`, `H27`, `H28` and `H29` (figure 8). They are
  `cause/narrow.py`'s group *many*, by its `held` and `group`, called, over all
  29 rules. The grouping reads the manual's regions, and no truth.
- **`whole_D`.** Over an arm's runs pooled, of the first-call drafts at impasses
  whose ticket a rule of `D` governs, those that name the right queue and a valid
  rule, the share classed *whole*.
- **`born_W(r)`.** Of run `r`'s first-call drafts at its impasses classed
  *whole*, the share whose escalation ended with the rule born.
- **The median** of three runs is the middle one, and of two runs their mean.

**The unit, *thin*, and when a row has no value.** All of it is settled here and
not in the scorer, which is where `PLAN_AUTHORSHIP.md`'s `E-c` lost its verdict.

- **Every adjudicated row but `IM-b` reads a median over runs.** `IM-b` pools
  v1n's runs, because a run meets few impasses at the defaults: v1c met 3 to 11
  a run (figure 3).
- **Every adjudicated row is a band reading.** Three runs an arm make three
  pairs, and *"With three pairs a sign test cannot reach 1/20, whatever the
  effect's size"* (`IDEAS.md`, *What the technical review of November 11, 2026
  proposes*). So no row here is a test. A verdict says on which side of its line
  a median fell, and `IM-g` gives each row's statistic run for run beside it.
- **A verdict on a median is labelled *thin*** when any one run of an arm the
  row reads, put in place of that arm's median, would put the statistic on the
  other side of its line. That is `PLAN_MANUAL.md`'s test, as
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` and `PLAN_WHOLE.md` applied it. **`IM-b`'s verdict is
  labelled *thin*** when leaving any one of v1n's runs out would put its share
  on the other side. The label does not change the verdict.
- **What a spread between runs as wide as a line does.**
  - v1c's bases decided 0.9510 to 0.9683 of the space in `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s
    period, and 0.9160 to 0.9222 in `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s. Within a period that is
    under two hundredths. But a base that loses a sixth of `SECURITY_INCIDENT`
    loses 8,400 points, 0.0625 of the space, and every v1c base of
    `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s period did (§3). So `IM-c` and `IM-d` may be thin
    whichever way they fall.
  - v1c's `whole_I` ran from 10/36 to 13/27 over the six runs (figure 2), a
    spread of about a fifth, and within a period from 10/36 to 13/27 and from
    11/36 to 14/31. That is as wide as `IM-a`'s line, so `IM-a` may be thin
    too.
  - No line was widened to absorb a spread, and none will be.
- **A run is complete** when it reaches case 2,000, or the cap of §1.
  - A run that cannot be completed leaves its arm with fewer runs. The rows read
    the runs that exist, and the record names the missing one.
  - A row that reads an arm with fewer than two runs has no value.
  - **`IM-a` reads the runs that have a `whole_I(r)`.** A run with no
    right-queue first-call draft at an impasse has none. With fewer than two
    such runs in either arm, the row has no value.
  - **`IM-b` has no value** if v1n's runs hold fewer than five right-queue
    first-call drafts at the defaults' impasses between them. [Amended before
    signature, 2026-11-28: the draft said ten; *What writing the package showed*,

  - **`IM-f` reads v1n's runs that have a whole first-call draft at an

- **A row with no value is unadjudicable**, and the record writes its verdict

- **A run that meets a long refusal for the rate** waits it out and, once every
  wait is spent, stops unrecorded; the same command resumes it from its partial

- **What the runs' commits may show.** The supervisor commits each record with a

  - **What may show:** each run's escalations in all, its calls, its verdicts by

F. **The order of Stage B is §9's**, and the module refuses a run out of it.

G. **The provider is pinned on every call.** A call that another provider served

## 5. The protocol

### 5.1 v1c, unchanged

`PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` §5.2, called and not changed: `placement/protocol.py`'s list
and escalation, `placement/loop.py`'s loop, `placement/run.py`'s proposer and
`placement/protocol.py`'s asker with its pin, under `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at
coverage 1/2. **`IM-g1` holds this plan's v1c to that plan's** on its three v1c
runs' own answers, and to `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s three v1c runs' too.

### 5.2 v1n: the first call at an impasse

At an escalation whose cause is an IMPASSE, v1c with the first call's user
message built over `PLAN_CAUSE.md`'s arm `i`'s paragraph in place of v2b's
`BLIND_BASE`. The paragraph is `cause.prompts.PARAGRAPHS["i"]`, whose SHA-256
begins `24b1b5c42568dd32`: v2b's `BLIND_BASE`, whose begins `8de0ebf50ce9a54b`,
a newline, and the notice, whose begins `4214ee3f23f383f9`. Its bytes are these,
with a final newline:

```text
BASE DE REGLAS: no se te muestra en este paso. Escribe tu regla para este
ticket; si se solapa con reglas existentes de OTRA cola, se te listaran despues.

AVISO DEL MOTOR: ninguna regla de la base casa este ticket. Por eso no ha podido
decidirlo.
```

- **Where it enters.** The user message is `rung2.proposers2.user_msg` over the
  paragraph: the paragraph, then *TICKET EN IMPASSE* and the ticket, as
  `PLAN_CAUSE.md`'s arm `i` asked it.
- **How the proposer knows the cause.** v1n's escalation calls
  `placement.protocol.propose_v1c` with a proposer that has been told, before its
  first call, whether the escalation's cause is an IMPASSE. The flag is read off
  the cause `channel.loop.escalates` returns, and is false at a CONFLICT and at
  an ERROR alike. It is cleared after the escalation.
- **The placement call** repeats the conversation: the system prompt, the same
  first user message, the draft as the assistant's turn, and v1c's placement
  message. So at an impasse it carries the notice too.
- **At a CONFLICT and at an ERROR** v1n's two calls send v1c's messages, byte for
  byte.
- **The other three texts are v1c's**, byte for byte: the system prompt, the
  placement message and the retry message. `IM-g2` holds each to v1c's by digest.
- **What the record keeps of it.** The header's `texts_sha16` gives v1c's four
  texts' digests and the paragraph's, under `notice_base`, and the fingerprint
  over the five. The record keeps v2b's paragraph as `base_text`, the notice's
  paragraph as `notice_text`, and the cases whose first call carried it as
  `notice_at`. A
  partial file's rows carry each request's digest, so a v1n run cannot be served
  an answer a v1c request paid for, nor the reverse.
- **A blocking check holds v1n to v1c**, call for call, on `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s
  and `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s six v1c runs (§7).

### 5.3 The trigger

Both arms run `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel arm, called through `placement/`.

- **What it is.** `channel.loop.escalates`, at coverage 1/2.
- **Its draws.** `channel.plan.draws("r{k}")` for run `k`, and
  `channel.plan.draws("smoke")` for the smoke run.
- **What it does.** A wrong decision whose draw is below 1/2 escalates as an
  `ERROR`. One whose draw is 1/2 or above stands, and its rule is counted as
  fired.

### 5.4 What a run records, and how it resumes

- **What it records** is `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` §5.4's record, with this plan's
  header, the notice's text and digest, and the cases at which it was sent.
- **How it resumes** is `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md` §5.5's, with both its signed
  amendments, called and not copied.
  - **A call refused for the rate on every attempt is waited out**, after each
    wait of `RATE_LIMIT_WAIT`.
  - **Once every wait is spent, it is an outage.** The run stops unrecorded, and
    the same command resumes from the git-ignored partial file.
  - **Five failed calls in a row** stop the run the same way.
  - **A call served from the partial file keeps the seconds the file kept.**
- **The partial file's header carries the protocol, the texts' digests and the
  draws**, so a run cannot resume under the other arm or other draws.
- **A run's span is taken from its first launch**, by the hour the first answer
  was kept, since a resumed record's `started_at` is its last launch's.
- **All of it is tested before the first run**, on runs built by hand.

### 5.5 The references Stage A computes

- **The drafting reading**, §0's figures 1 to 6, recomputed from
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s and `PLAN_WHOLE.md`'s records by `impasse/references.py`,
  and figure 8 from the text's regions.
- **The transcriber** under v1c and every draw, `placement/references.py`'s,
  called. It writes the governing rule's region, and ignores the text either arm
  carries, so its figures under v1n are its figures under v1c.

## 6. Known traps

**6.1 — Every error escalation is told it is an impasse.** v1 calls the model the
system's exception component, and the user message labels the ticket *TICKET EN
IMPASSE*. On an error escalation both are false, in both arms. At an impasse
v1n's notice agrees with them.

**6.2 — Level 3 hands over the label** (`PLAN_MANUAL.md` §6.2). Every row
measures what the loop compiles from a policy it holds as text.

**6.3 — The trigger reads the truth.** In a deployment the report comes from the
world. Here the corpus labels stand in for it.

**6.4 — The online figures are corrected online.** A reported error's decision is
replaced by the proposer's answer. They are reported, and no row reads them.

**6.5 — The draws fix which cases can be reported, not which are.** The bases part
from the first impasse one arm drafts whole and the other cuts.

**6.6 — `SECURITY_INCIDENT` weighs 3/8 of the space.** `space(B)` moves with its
recall more than with any other queue's, and one lost sixth of it is 8,400
points. Every space figure in `IM-g` is split by the keyword.

**6.7 — The notice could leak the policy in code.** It names neither a queue nor a
rule, and it reaches the proposer through the frozen route only.
`tests/test_oracle_separation.py` watches the package's proposer-path modules.

**6.8 — A whole draft of a default is the default.** At a ticket `H29` governs,
the region whole is the catch-all. It is born, and containment puts every
narrower rule above it, as the transcriber's run does. A whole `H03`, `H08` or
`H27` holds rules of other queues too, and beats them where they are not yet
born.

**6.9 — `whole_I` reads the first call, not the birth.** A whole draft that is
refused counts in `IM-a`, and its refusal counts in `IM-f`.

**6.10 — The notice limits its own reach.** A region drafted whole takes later
impasses away, so v1n's late impasses are those its earlier whole drafts did not
cover. `IM-a` reads v1n's impasses as they came, and `IM-e` counts them.

**6.11 — At the defaults the notice is read as the default missing**
(`FINDINGS_CAUSE.md`, B and C). The model writes a rule nested in it, kept to
its ticket's own combination. Such a rule decides its own points, and the rest of
the default's region stays open to later impasses. `IM-b` reads the drafts, and
`IM-g` counts the impasses at the defaults.

**6.12 — `H27` has never been asked under the notice.** It governs none of
`PLAN_HALVES.md`'s tickets, and no v1c or v1d run met an impasse at its tickets
(figure 8). It is in `D` by the reading's own count.

**6.13 — The notice is never sent at the rarest queue's tickets** on any run on
record (said first). `IM-g` reports the queue by cause, draft and verdict, and
each run beside the transcriber under its own draws.

**6.14 — A whole draft cannot take back what a narrower rule of another queue
holds.** A rule of another queue born before it, whose extension lies strictly
inside the draft's, beats it there by containment, right or wrong: *"Narrow ≠
correct"* (`ARBITRATION_REPORT.md` §2). v1c's round never lists such a rule.

**6.15 — An empty object is a draft naming no rule.** DeepInfra's runs got one on
dozens of calls (`IDEAS.md`, the technical review of 2026-11-11). It parses, is
not asked again, and is left out of `whole_I`'s denominator. `IM-g` counts them
by arm and by cause.

**6.16 — A median of three runs moves on one run.** *Thin* says so, as §0 sets,
and every row is a band reading.

**6.17 — Back-to-back runs leave `git_dirty` true**, because each finds the
earlier records not yet committed. The supervisor commits each record before the
next run, and `code_dirty` is what identifies the code.

**6.18 — A third period for v1c.** This plan's v1c runs are `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s
arm on new dates, on the same provider. Between its two earlier periods its
median `space(B)` moved from 0.9674 to 0.9192 (§3). Every row reads v1c in this
plan's own period, and `IM-g` sets its runs beside the earlier ones under the
same draws.

**6.19 — Asked about two rules, the model favoured the broader one** (`EXTERNAL_REVIEW.md`
§2), on the pairwise thread's question, which carried no manual. A whole draft is
the broader rule of each crossing pair its round lists, so v1n's rounds may
declare it above rules the policy puts first. `IM-g` reads the placed edges'
direction against the better rule, as `RL-e` read it, in both arms.

**[Added before signature, 2026-11-28] Found writing the package.**

- **A resumed run rebuilds `notice_at` as it was.** The answers a resume serves
