## 5. The protocol, v2e: v2 with v1e's discipline, asked only where queues differ

**Everything not named here is v2 as it produced rung 2's records, and v1e's

fingerprint, and Sergi may change it before signing and not after.

**5.1 — The paragraph on what to declare.** v2's paragraph on overlap keeps its
first part, that overlap is normal and that a rule is not to be narrowed to dodge
another, and its last, that the set arithmetic is the engine's. Its middle
sentence, *"Si tu regla es un caso particular de otra (todo caso que casa la tuya
casa tambien la otra), no declares nada: el nivel 1 ya lo resuelve solo."*, is
replaced by:

> *Tienes que situar tu regla frente a TODA regla existente con la que se
> solape y que mande los tickets a OTRA cola, la contenga o no: si tu regla es
> una EXCEPCION a ella, de modo que la tuya manda donde casen las dos, ponla en
> `beats`; si es un caso GENERAL por debajo de ella, de modo que la otra manda,
> ponla en `loses_to`. Si una de las dos contiene a la otra, el nivel 1 ya hace
> ganar a la mas contenida: declararlo lo confirma, declarar lo contrario lo
> contradice, y el motor lo registrara. Frente a una regla de tu MISMA cola no
> declares nada: donde se pisan deciden lo mismo. Una regla que se solape con
> otra de distinta cola sin situarse frente a ella se te devolvera con la lista
> de las que faltan.*

v1e's paragraph said only what happens when the new rule is contained in

had the new rule as the container. This one says which of the two wins either

**5.2 — The validator: v1e's, with one change.** `O` is the set of existing

> *Tu regla se solapa con estas reglas existentes de OTRA cola, frente a las que

> una en `beats` o `loses_to`, o cambia la regla.*

A declaration against a rule of the same queue is not asked for, and is not

**5.3 — The order answer, on a CONFLICT only.** v1e's, word for word, §5.3 of

## 6. Known traps

**6.1 — The protocol is a bundle.** The framing, the arithmetic, the exemption,
the discipline, the order answer and the copy refusal change together against
v1, and three of them against v1e. The rows read the bundle. O-d's split by
channel is what the record can still separate.

**6.2 — The arithmetic has failed once.** With it in front of it, the proposer
still cited rules it did not overlap (`CHAT_SUMMARY.md`'s erratum to §2.2). Half
of v1e's declarations were refused as `no_solapan`. O-d reports that share again,
and a fall is not assumed.

**6.3 — A proposer can still obey by partitioning.** The exemption frees only
overlaps within a queue. **A fall in CONFLICTs is not a success**: conflict is
the alarm, and partitioning removes the detector (`ARBITRATION_REPORT.md` §3,
level 3). O-a is the row that reads it.

**6.4 — Idle edges can still be installed.** A declaration against a rule of the
same queue that overlaps without nesting enters the graph and changes nothing,
except that it can close a cycle against a useful one. O-d counts both.

**6.5 — The screen grows.** v2's rendering and the repair rounds put more in
front of the model, and `FINDINGS_FIDELITY.md` found a sign that a screen of
compiled rules costs it accuracy. The proposer's action accuracy is reported
beside both baselines', and a fall is read as a possible cost of the screen.

**6.6 — The date.** v1 was asked on 2026-09-30 and v1e on 2026-10-05. Every
comparison names both dates.

**6.7 — The listing order.** A repair round's list is shuffled with a fixed seed
and the order recorded, as v1e's was. It adjudicates nothing.

**6.8 — The corpus room is a handful of cases.** O-b is read over the space for
that reason, as `E-c` was, and its corpus reading is reported.

**6.9 — Read whole, not as it grew.** The rows read each final base from case 0.
What the loop decided while the base grew is O-d's.

**6.10 — Level 1 is not overridable here.** A declaration that a narrower rule
loses to a rule containing it is refused as `contradice_subsuncion` and recorded.
Letting declarations override subsumption would be another engine and another
plan.

**6.11 — The rarest queue.** The escalation trigger is v1's, and `U-d` found it
never asks about `ONCALL_ESCALATION`. O-d counts it again and expects zero.

## 11. The gate, and where the code lives

**Every module that writes this plan's records refuses while §0 is unsigned**,
the free ones too, and `overlap/run.py` refuses before it constructs the client.
**The free stage gets a gate because what it protects is not money**
(`PLAN_SENSITIVITY.md` §8): Stage C's figures are what the rows are, and without
the gate only commit order stands between them and a band.

**It reads `PLAN_OVERLAP.md` and no other plan, and it counts signatures**: every
line starting `**Signed by Sergi:` must be filled in, and there must be at least
one. If a blocking check forces an amendment, the amendment carries its own
signature line. No flag skips the gate; `--dry-run` runs `O-g1` to `O-g3` and
writes nothing.

**Constants fixed here, before any figure exists, not to be tuned afterwards and
pinned by tests:** `N = 2000`, `SEED = 17`, `MODEL =
"deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash"`, `REASONING = {"effort": "none"}`, `PROMPT =
"v2e"`, `MAX_SHOWN = 12`, `REPAIR_ROUNDS = 1`, `LISTING_SEED = 17`, `REPS = 3`,
`SMOKE_N = 20`, unless the signature says otherwise; and §0's lines, `0.50`,
`0.50` with its minimum of two runs with room, and `0.70`.

Drafter's proposal for the layout; the naming is Sergi's to overrule:

```
overlap/__init__.py
overlap/plan.py       the gate, the constants, §0's lines

                      validator, the fingerprint; authorship's proposer extended
```

**The plumbing goes in the same commit as the modules.** `overlap` joins

## 13. What this plan cannot settle, declared before it runs

1. **The framing apart from the discipline.** Without §2's control arm, a base
   that keeps overlapping could owe it to v2's framing alone.
2. **The requirement apart from the paragraph that states it.** The protocol
   asks and enforces together, as v1e did.
3. **The arithmetic apart from the rest.** v2's rendering comes with the bundle,
   and its effect is not separated.
4. **The material.** No declaration changes which queue the model writes, and
   `T3_ENGINEERING` and `ACCOUNT_MANAGER` keep their missing rules (item (d)
   of `CHAT_SUMMARY.md`'s erratum to §4). `E-b` was refuted for that reason,
   and it is not carried here.
5. **A trigger that would ask about the rarest queue.** v1's trigger is kept, and
   none is specified.
6. **Whether level 1 should yield to a declaration.** O-d reads every refused
   one against the truth. It does not ask what overriding subsumption would buy.
7. **One model, one corpus, `R` draws.** `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash`, as every
   paid record.
