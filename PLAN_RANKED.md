**The item.** `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md` found that compiling a run's corrections at
once beats the loop that received them one by one (`CR-d` holds). Its free
readings then said where the loop loses them, and that most of what it lacks it
had already declared.

- **In two runs of three the loop lost by its order, not by its rules.** An
  order of the loop's own rules, found with the truth, decides more of the space
  than at once in L r1 and L r2. In L r3 no order reaches it
  (`results_corrections/FINDINGS_CORRECTIONS.md`, *Shape or order*).
- **That order is mostly one ranking of the eight queues**, `SECURITY_INCIDENT`
  first (*A ranking of the queues as the order*).
- **The loop had declared most of it.** At every birth the placement round's
  answer puts the newborn above or below rules of other queues. Gathered by pair
  of queues, each run's answers choose one ranking. It orders the run's final
  rules better than the base the loop installed, in every run (*The ranking the
  loop declared*).
- **The engine refused the righter votes.** It installs a declaration as an edge
  between two rules, and refuses one that closes a cycle. The declarations it
  refused agree with the best ranking more often than those it installed.
- **Replayed on the loop's own births, the ranking keeps what conflicts cost.**
  Ranked after each birth by the declarations so far, the base ends above the
  record in every run. It still falls where a rule of another queue is born
  nested in the keyword's region, which no ranking undoes (*The ranking the loop
  declared, as it went*).
- **Every one of those readings is a replay.** Its rules are the ones L wrote
  under rung 2's order. A loop that ranked as it went would decide other cases,
  receive other reports and write other rules. Only calls can say what it
  compiles.

**The question, in its cleanest form.** With level 1's catalogue in the prompt,
reasoning on, `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at coverage 1/2, v1c's placement round
and a report that carries the true queue:

- does a loop whose engine ranks its base by its own placement votes, as it
  goes, decide more of the function than the same loop under rung 2's order?
- does it keep `SECURITY_INCIDENT`'s region, which that loop lost?
- does it reach what compiling its corrections at once decides?
- does it inherit the one pair its votes had wrong?
- does it compile the rarest queue?

**Two arms.** Both are `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md`'s arm L: `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s v1c
at level 1, with reasoning on, under `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at coverage 1/2,
at n=2000 and seed 17, with report R1 at a reported error.

- **L, the control**, is that arm, called and not changed. Its engine is rung
  2's: a rule strictly inside another beats it, a declared edge orders two
  crossing rules, and an edge that closes a cycle is refused. Undefeated rules of
  two queues are a CONFLICT, which escalates.
- **K, the ranked arm**, is L with one change: **what decides a case.**
  - **The votes.** Every citation of a rule of another queue, in the answer of
    every rule born so far, is one vote between the two queues: for the
    newborn's queue where it declared itself above, and against it where below.
    Every vote counts alike, whatever rung 2's engine did with the edge.
  - **The ranking.** Of the 40,320 rankings of the eight queues, the engine
    takes the one that agrees with the most votes, and the earliest of those
    that tie.
  - **The order.** The base is ordered by that ranking, with every rule strictly
    inside another before it: `corrections/hierarchy.py`'s pulled construction.
    A case is decided by the first rule of that order that matches it.
  - **When.** The ranking and the order are taken again after every birth.
  - **What follows.** The order is total, so K's engine never returns CONFLICT.
    A case escalates on an impasse, or on a reported error.
- **Everything the proposer is handed is L's**, built by the same code on the
  same structures: the system prompt, the first call, the report, v1c's list and
  the placement message. K's engine still installs each answer's edges as rung
  2 does, because v1c's list and the placement message read them. No decision of
  K reads them (§2.2).
- **The runs.** Each arm has three runs, and the arms alternate. Run `k` of each
  arm reads the draws of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s c r`k`, as `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md`'s
  runs did, and every call goes to one provider.

**Set beside each run, on its own labels**, as `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md` set them
beside L's:

- **At once.** Under level 1's compile prompt, with reasoning on, the model is
  handed the labelled tickets of a run and asked for the whole base in one
  answer, at checkpoints along the run and at its end (§2.6).
- **The learners**, free: `PLAN_ILP.md`'s inducer and `PLAN_STUDENTS.md`'s
  students on the same labelled tickets. Reported.
- **Two readings of each run under the other arm's engine**, free and reported:
  - K's final base as rung 2's engine reads it, by containment and the edges it
    installed;
  - L's base ranked by its own votes as it went, which is
    `corrections/online.py`'s replay on this period's L runs.

**What it changes in what is measured, said first.**

- **The arms differ in the engine's decision, and in nothing the proposer is
  handed.** What follows from the decision differs too: which cases escalate,
  which reports come, and so which rules are written. That is the effect the
  plan buys, and no row separates its parts. The two free readings above are
  replays, not arms.
- **K's engine never abstains where a rule matches.** Rung 2's engine returns
  CONFLICT where undefeated rules disagree, and the loop asks. K's decides
  there, and asks only if the decision is wrong and the channel reports it.
  - **What L asked there.** In `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md`'s L runs, 125, 118 and 74
    escalations were conflicts, of 377, 368 and 348.
  - **What that costs K.** Half of its wrong decisions at such points stay
    silent. `ARBITRATION_REPORT.md`'s design rule and what this departs from
    are in the paragraph on the three documents, above.
- **What the proposer is told about the engine is no longer what K's engine
  does.** The system prompt says that where two rules cross and neither is
  declared above the other, the engine *"seguira sin saberlo y escalara cada vez
  que ambas casen"*. K's engine ranks them and decides. The text is kept byte
  for byte, so that the arms differ in the engine alone. Whether telling the
  proposer would change its votes is another plan (§13).
- **The proposer declares between rules, and K reads it between queues.** The
  placement message asks whether the newborn is an exception to each listed
  rule. K counts each answer as a vote on the two rules' queues. The free
  readings read the closed records the same way, and the bands come from them.
- **Rung 2's engine was designed against a global order.** Its docstring says
  an integer *"demands a GLOBAL decision from the proposer based on a LOCAL
  observation"*, and that it *"COMPETES"* with subsumption where a reference
  *"COMPOSES"* with it.
  - **K asks the proposer for nothing global.** It gathers the local references
    into one ranking of queues, by a count.
  - **Where the ranking and containment disagree, containment wins**: the pulled
    construction puts the nested rule first. So the ranking does not undo a rule
    of another queue born inside a queue's region.
- **A ranking of queues cannot hold the policy whole.** The hidden policy's own
  29 rules, under their best ranking, decide 0.9756 of the space
  (`FINDINGS_CORRECTIONS.md`, *A ranking of the queues as the order*, section
  3). That is the instrument's ceiling, and §7 makes it a blocking check. It is
  not ~100%, and the plan says so here rather than after the runs.
- **The label reaches the ranking through the proposer.** K's ranking reads no
  label and no truth. At a reported error the proposer is told the true queue,
  and its answer's citations are votes. So the channel's labels move the
  ranking, by one route, and §0's table says so.
- **The votes are not the replay's.** In L an installed edge settled its two
  rules, and their region erred no more. In K a pair of queues the ranking has
  wrong keeps erring, and each reported error casts votes against it. How much
  that moves the ranking depends on how often arrivals exercise the pair, which
  §0 counts for the one pair a row reads.
- **Before the first vote the ranking is the domain's order of the queues**,
  `harness/domain.py`'s `ACTIONS`: `T1_GENERAL` first and `SECURITY_INCIDENT`
  fifth. It is the replay's tie rule, which reads no truth and is arbitrary.
  The record counts the decisions taken on a tie.
- **The surfaces.**
  - **The space is the out-of-sample surface**, and every adjudicated row reads
    it. It weighs the keyword's half as half of its points, and 71 of the 2,000
    arrivals carry the keyword: the tickets of `H01`, `H02` and `H03` in
    `results_channel/sample.json`.
  - **The arrivals are in-sample** wherever a label was read. The corpus figure
    is split into the labelled tickets and the rest, and the online figure, each
    decision as it was taken, is given beside them.
- **Every row reads within one period and one provider.** `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md`'s
  own L runs, of 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-14, are another period. They are set
  beside this plan's runs in `RK-g`, and no row reads them.
- **It keeps the oracle's separation as `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md` left it**: broken
  on purpose, by that plan's one route, in both arms.

## 0. Predictions — bands and refutation lines

Drafted, unsigned. One row is one event. **A band's edge is its own refutation
line**, so band and refutation partition the axis and nothing can fall between
them.

**The runs.** `R = 3` runs per arm.
- **The loop.** Each run is `PLAN_REUSE.md`'s loop over the 2,000 cases of
  `generate_corpus(2000, seed=17)`, with a fresh engine of its arm, under
  `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at coverage 1/2.
- **The draws.** Run `k` of either arm reads the draws of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s
  `c r{k}`.
- **The order.** The arms alternate, as §2.5 sets.

**The statistics.** Each is a ratio of integer counts, a difference of such
ratios, or a count of runs. **Each is compared with its line exactly, as a
fraction.** No rounding enters a verdict.

- **`B`.** A run's final base, rebuilt from its record.
- **`space(B)` and `recall_q(B)`** are read as the run's own engine decides.
  - **In L** they are `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s, computed by the same functions.
  - **In K** the base is put in the order the engine held after its last birth,
    and a point is decided by the first rule of that order that matches it.
  - **In both**, a point the base leaves undecided counts as wrong.
- **`ranking(r)`.** The ranking K's engine held after the last birth of run
  `r`. It is recomputed from the record's rules, and §7 holds the two to each
  other.
  - **How it weighs a vote.** Every citation of a born rule's answer counts
    alike, whether or not its two rules decide a point between them (§2.3).
  - **Where a pair's votes are wrong**, it follows them. `RK-e` reads one pair.
  - **How it meets containment.** Under the pulled construction: a rule nested
    in a rule of a higher-ranked queue comes just before it, and wins there
    (§2.4).
- **`labels(r)`.** The reported errors of run `r`, in arrival order, each a
  ticket and its true queue. In both arms they reach the proposer.
- **`A(r)`.** The at-once asks from all of `labels(r)`. `space(A(r))` is the
  median of their `space`, each read as `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md` §5.4 read them.
- **The median** of three runs is the middle one, and of two runs their mean.

**The unit, *thin*, and when a row has no value.** All of it is settled here and
not in the scorer.

- **`RK-b`, `RK-c`, `RK-d` and `RK-f` read medians.** With three runs an arm a
  sign test cannot reach 1/20, whatever the effect's size. So each is a band
  reading, as every loop plan since `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` read its rows. Six
  alternated pairs were not chosen (§2.5).
- **A verdict on a median is labelled *thin*** when any one run of an arm the row
  reads, put in place of that arm's median, would put the statistic on the other
  side of its line. That is `PLAN_MANUAL.md`'s test, as `PLAN_WHOLE.md` and
  `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md` applied it. The label does not change the verdict.
- **`RK-a` and `RK-e` read counts of runs**, because a ranking puts a queue
  first, or one queue above another, all at once or not at all
  (`PLAN_SPOTCHECK.md`'s lesson). *Thin* does not apply to them: the count is
  the reading.
- **A spread between an arm's runs as wide as the gap between the arms** is what
  the closed L runs showed: their `space(B)` spans 0.1426. *Thin* is how a row
  says so. The drafter expects `RK-b` and `RK-c` to be thin whichever way they
  fall.
- **A run is complete** when it reaches case 2,000, or the cap of §1.
  - A run at the cap is complete, and its base is read as it stands.
  - A run that cannot be completed leaves its arm with fewer runs. The rows read
    the runs that exist, and the record names the missing one.
  - A row that reads an arm with fewer than two runs has no value.
  - **`RK-a` reads every complete K run**, and holds only if all of them put the
    queue first.
  - **`RK-e` holds on two runs**, of three or of two.
  - **`RK-d` has no value for a run without a valid at-once ask** from all of its
    labels, after the re-asks §2.6 allows.
  - **A K run with no vote cast** has the domain's order for its ranking. It is
    read as it stands, and the record says so.
- **A row with no value is unadjudicable**, and the record says so.

## 5. The protocol

*This section says what the package is held to. It is completed against the
package before the signature.*

### 5.1 L: `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md`'s arm L

Called, with nothing changed: `corrections.loop.run_loop` in arm `l`, on a fresh
`rung2.engine2.PriorityEngine`, with that plan's proposer, texts, report and
route of the label. `RK-g1` holds it to that plan's three L records, served
their answers.

### 5.2 K: the same loop on the ranked engine

K's run is the same call with one argument changed: the engine. It is a
subclass of `PriorityEngine` that overrides `decide`, and changes nothing else
it inherits.
- **What it inherits.** `add`, `try_edge`, containment, the installed edges and
  the edge log. `placement.protocol.propose_v1c` reads them as it reads L's.
- **Its votes** are read from the born rules as
  `corrections/declared.py`'s `citations` reads a record's: `beats`, `loses_to`
  and `dropped_edges`, a rule and a side.
- **Its ranking** is the earliest, in `itertools.permutations(ACTIONS)`, of the
  rankings that agree with the most votes, as `corrections/online.py`'s
  `Agreement` chooses it.
- **Its order** is `corrections.hierarchy.pulled` over the base's containment
  and that ranking.
- **Its decision.** No rule matches: IMPASSE. Otherwise ACTION, by the first
  rule of the order that matches. It never returns CONFLICT.
- **When it ranks.** Before deciding a case, if the base has grown since the
  last decision. A birth's citations are complete by then.

`channel.loop.escalates`, called: an IMPASSE or a CONFLICT escalates, and a
decision other than the case's label escalates as an error when its draw falls

### 5.4 The at-once asks, and how an answer is read

## 6. Known traps

- **K's record holds edges no decision read.** A reader that rebuilds K's base
  and decides with rung 2's engine reads another machine. `space(B)` in K is
  the ranked order's, and §0 says so. The scorer of this package reads each arm
  by its own engine, and a test pins that the two readings of one K base
  differ where they should.
- **The system prompt describes rung 2's engine.** It says an undeclared
  crossing escalates every time. In K it does not.
- **A decision K takes is not a decision K would take at the end.** The ranking
  moves as votes come. The online figure reads each decision as it was taken,
  and `space(B)` reads the last order.
- **The early ranking is the domain's order**, `T1_GENERAL` first, until votes
  come. The record counts the decisions taken on a tie.
- **A vote is counted once a citation, not once an answer.** An answer that
  places its newborn against twelve listed rules of one queue casts twelve
  votes. `PLAN_OVERLAP.md`'s lesson is that a rate over declared edges can rest
  on one declaration. §2.3 keeps the count the replay read, and `RK-g` reports
  the votes by answer beside the votes by citation.
- **A correction may not decide its own ticket.** A rule nested inside it, often
  the rule that erred, keeps the ticket by containment, in K as in L. `RK-g`
  counts it after every birth, as `CLAUDE.md` asks of a loop that hands the
  proposer corrections.
- **A rule of another queue nested in the keyword's region** takes
  `SECURITY_INCIDENT`'s points whatever the ranking says. `RK-c` reads what is
  left.
- **K may spin.** A pair of queues the ranking has wrong keeps erring, and each
  report writes another correction that loses. That is how a K run could reach
  the cap.
- **"Level 1" means three things in this tree.** See the preamble.
- **The labelled tickets are each arm's own errors.** They differ between the
  arms, so `A(r)` is asked of different labels in K and in L.
- **An at-once answer is a list**, read in its own order in both arms. It is
  not ranked by K's engine.
- **One ticket's story can mislead.** Check it against the run's other tickets of
  its region before citing it.
- **A median of three runs moves on one run.** *Thin* says so, as §0 sets.

## 13. What this plan cannot settle, declared before it runs

1. **Which part of K's effect is the order, and which the rules it leads to.**
   The arms differ in the decision, and everything after it follows. The two
   cross-readings of `RK-g` are replays.
2. **Whether telling the proposer how K's engine ranks would change its votes.**
   The system prompt describes rung 2's engine in both arms.
3. **A rule of another queue born nested in a queue's region.** A ranking does
   not undo containment, and a guard that reads no truth takes the loop's own
   exceptions with its errors. The lever left is in the births: a first call
   shown the rule that erred. That is another plan.
4. **Another count of the votes**: one weighted by what each pair decides, or
   one vote an answer. Either would be set before its own run.
5. **An engine that ranks only where its votes speak**, and returns CONFLICT
   for a pair of queues with no vote, or an even one. K ranks every pair, on a
   tie rule.
6. **The ranking without labels.** K runs at level 1 with report R1. The same
   engine under arm N's configuration, or at level 3, is not run.
7. **Whether the report or the label carries what L and K share.**
   `PLAN_CORRECTIONS.md` §13's first item stands.
8. **What no arrival exercises.** The votes come where rules cross at a birth,
   and the errors where arrivals fall. A pair of queues the arrivals seldom
   exercise is ranked by few votes.
9. **A paired effect.** Three pairs of runs cannot carry a sign test. Every row
   is a band reading.
10. **One model, one provider, one manual level, one coverage, one arrival
    order, and three runs an arm.**
