**Status: drafted by Claude on 2026-11-08, unsigned.** Under hard rule 2 of
`CLAUDE.md` a model may draft a band and may not sign it. **Nothing runs and no
record is written until Sergi has signed §0.** The signature has to land before

**Asked for by Sergi on 2026-11-08**, after four POST-RUN readings of
`PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s records. [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md) carries it under *What
`PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` opens*, *Why the loop writes narrow rules*: *a text that asks
for the governing rule's region whole would test it. Another plan.* The readings
are [`results_placement/FINDINGS_PLACEMENT.md`](results_placement/FINDINGS_PLACEMENT.md)'s
four POST-RUN sections. They reached `main` in #211, #213, #215 and #217.

**The provider follows from the design** by the rule agreed with Sergi on

arm runs inside the plan, so DeepInfra's fp8 endpoint is pinned. No row sets a
run against another provider's, so there is no bridge (§2.6). It is his to sign.

**The item.** The loop writes its narrow rules as cuts, and the cuts cost the
rarest queue.

- **The cut.** Where a ticket matches, after its governing rule, a listed rule
  whose region does not contain the governing rule's, about seven narrow births
  in ten are the governing rule's region cut by one listed rule.
  - It is 118 of 167 in `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s six runs on DeepInfra, and 124 of
    165 and 69 of 102 in `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s and `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`'s on
    Baidu.
  - Nine cuts in ten, about, are by the first such rule, and by a rule of another
    queue (`FINDINGS_PLACEMENT.md`, *Whether the cut is why the loop's births are
    narrow*).
- **Why the proposer cuts.** Level 3's system prompt says: *"Por eso solo
  necesitas declarar prioridad frente a reglas que se solapan con la tuya sin
  que una contenga a la otra. Si tu regla es un caso particular de otra, no
  declares nada: el nivel 1 ya lo resuelve."* A cut is a particular case of the
  lower rule it would conflict with.
  - The notes at the rarest queue's tickets say so. In translation, at case 1432
    of v1c r3: *"Automatic containment prioritises it over H04 and H07, without
    any need to declare priority"* (`FINDINGS_PLACEMENT.md`, *Why the runs under
    run 3's draws lost `ONCALL_ESCALATION`*).
- **What it costs.** A cut decides only its own points.
  - At a rule whose tickets escalate often, a later draft writes the region
    whole. Of `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s 80 rule-runs with a cut, 58 also had a whole
    draft born, and lost 4,445 points of the space.
  - At a rule whose tickets escalate one to three times a run, the cut is the
    region's only draft. The 10 rule-runs where every draft was a cut lost
    15,720 points (`FINDINGS_PLACEMENT.md`, *What the cuts cost*).
  - **Under v1c that is the rarest queue.** `H04`'s and `H05`'s points are 12,560
    of the 15,215 v1c's three bases lose. Outside them a base loses under one
    point in a hundred of the space.
- **So the rarest queue is lost at the first call.** Under run 3's draws the
  channel supplied the queue, and the proposer drafted the right queue at every
  escalation of it. No draft at a ticket of `H04` was `H04`'s region whole, in
  eleven. On the bridge, both providers cut at two of the queue's tickets, and
  DeepInfra cut at two more where Baidu did not (`FINDINGS_PLACEMENT.md`,
  *Whether the cut is the provider's*).

**None of this is new analysis, and the plan's part is the instrument.** The four
readings are the analysis, and they are POST-RUN. What this plan adds is a first
call that asks for the region whole, run beside v1c's, with bands signed before
any of its figures exists.

**What the drafter found before drafting, and why this design.** All of it is
free, and none of it reads an answer this plan buys. §0 gives the figures and
their provenance.

- **v1c's first calls write the part whole about two times in five.** In
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s v1c runs, of the first-call drafts of the right queue,
  35 of 92, 19 of 64 and 35 of 85 hold their ticket's part whole, a median of
  0.3804. v1b's runs: 38 of 79, 39 of 82 and 27 of 73.
- **A whole draft would meet longer lists.** Each v1c base was rebuilt as it
  stood before each recorded escalation, and v1c's list was taken for the
  governing rule's region whole.
  - The median list is 9, 5.5 and 6 rules by run, against 5, 4 and 5 for the
    drafts that were sent. The longest is 35, 23 and 43.
  - At the rarest queue's tickets it is 14 to 30 rules.
  - Under v1c a crossing rule left without a side still refuses the birth. So
    the round is where this design can fail, and `WR-c` bands it.
- **A proposer that drafts the region whole compiles the queue where the draws
  supply it.** The transcriber writes the governing rule's region and places it
  by the list's order. Under v1c and each of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s three draws it
  recalls 1, 2/3 and 1 of `ONCALL_ESCALATION` over the space
  (`FINDINGS_PLACEMENT.md`, Stage A).

**The question, in its cleanest form.** With level 3's manual in the prompt,
reasoning on, the channel at coverage 1/2 and v1c's placement round:

- does a first call told to write the governing rule's region whole write it?
- does the loop then compile the rarest queue?
- are the whole drafts refused much more often, against longer lists?
- does it cost anything elsewhere, within one period?
- does v1c, run again beside it, still lose the rarest queue?

**Two arms.** Both run `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s v1c: `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel arm
with v1c's placement round. That is `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`'s loop on rung 2's
engine, at n=2000 and seed 17, with level 3's manual and reasoning on. A case
escalates on an IMPASSE or a CONFLICT, and on a wrong decision whose draw falls
below 1/2. Each arm has three runs, the arms alternate, and every call goes to
one provider.

- **v1c** is `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s arm, called and not changed.
- **v1d** is v1c with one change, to the first call's user message (§5.2). The
  paragraph that stands where the base would be, v2b's `BLIND_BASE`, carries the
  instruction §2.1 chooses. Every other text is v1c's, byte for byte: the system
  prompt, the placement message and the retry message.

**What it changes in what is measured, said first.**

- **The arms differ in one paragraph of the first call**, and in nothing else the
  proposer is handed. The placement round reads what the first call drafted,
  under v1c's rules in both arms.
- **At level 3 the instruction asks, in effect, for the list's own rule.** The
  governing rule's region is the transcriber's draft. So v1d measures whether the
  model, told, writes it, and whether the placement round then carries it. It
  does not measure what a proposer without the list would write, which no
  level-3 plan can.
- **The instruction and the system prompt pull two ways.** The system prompt's
  level-1 sentence stays, byte for byte. A whole draft is a case of no lower
  rule, so the sentence no longer covers it. How the proposer reads the two
  together is part of what is measured, and the notes are kept whole.
- **Whole drafts meet longer lists**, and v1c still refuses an answer that leaves
  a listed rule without a side. That rule is kept on purpose (§2.2). A run where
  the whole drafts are refused tells what the round costs a whole draft.
- **The pairing fixes which cases can be reported, not which are.** Run `k` of
  both arms reads one set of draws. The bases differ between the arms, and so do
  their wrong decisions.
- **Both arms break rung 2's invariant**, as `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s and
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s did. The trigger reads the truth of every arrival, and
  acts on half the wrong decisions. Neither the label nor the fact that the base
  erred reaches the proposer.
- **Reasoning is on**, as in `PLAN_REASONED_LOOP.md`, `PLAN_CHANNEL.md` and
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`, against `PLAN_REUSE.md` §1's amendment, for this plan's
  calls only. Without it the model does not find the first matching rule
  (`FM-c`), and a first call told to write that rule's region would read the
  wrong one.
- **Every row is read within one provider.** Every comparison with another plan's
  runs crosses dates, and is reported in `WR-f`.

## 0. Predictions — bands and refutation lines

Drafted, unsigned. One row is one event. **A band's edge is its own refutation
line**, so band and refutation partition the axis and nothing can fall between
them.

**The runs.** `R = 3` runs per arm. Each run is `PLAN_REUSE.md`'s loop over the
2,000 cases of `generate_corpus(2000, seed=17)`, with a fresh rung 2 engine. Its
trigger is `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s channel at coverage 1/2. Run `k` of either arm
reads the draws of `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s `c r{k}`, as `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s did. The
arms alternate, as §2.3 sets.

**The statistics.** Each is a ratio of integer counts. **Each is compared with its
line exactly, as a fraction.** No rounding enters a verdict.

- **`B`, `space(B)`, `recall_q(B)`, `calls(r)` and `refused(r)`** are
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s, computed by the same functions.
- **`whole(r)`.** Of run `r`'s first-call drafts of the right queue, the share
  that hold their ticket's part whole.
  - **A draft's part** is the points of its ticket's governing rule's region whose
    true queue is that rule's, as `placement/births.py` reads a birth's.
  - **A draft holds it** when every one of those points lies in the draft's
    extension. A draft wider than the part holds it too.
  - **Drafts of another queue, and drafts naming no valid rule,** are left out of
    the share, above and below. They are counted and reported.
  - **It is read on the first call's answer, born or not**, by
    `placement/cost.py`'s `draft_class`. The first call is what the text
    changes.
- **The median** of three runs is the middle one, and of two runs their mean.

**The unit, *thin*, and when a row has no value.** All of it is settled here and
not in the scorer.

- **Every adjudicated row reads medians over an arm's runs.**
- **A verdict on a median is labelled *thin*** when any one run of an arm the
  row reads, put in place of that arm's median, would put the statistic on the
  other side of its line. That is `PLAN_MANUAL.md`'s test, as
  `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` applied it. The label does not change the verdict.
- **What a spread between runs as wide as a line does.** `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s
  v1c bases decided 0.9510 to 0.9683 of the space, a spread of 0.0173. That is
  less than `WR-d`'s line of 1/40, so one run can move `WR-d` only near its line.
  Its v1c runs recalled 1/15 to 8/15 of the rarest queue, so `WR-e` may be thin
  whichever way it falls. No line was widened to absorb a spread, and none will
  be.
- **What one run does to `WR-b`.** The draws of run 2 make no `H05` ticket
  reportable, and `H05` governs a third of the queue's points. Under them the
  transcriber recalls 2/3 of the queue. Unless the proposer writes `H05`'s region
  from another ticket, `WR-b` holds only if v1d's runs 1 and 3 both reach its

- **A run is complete** when it reaches case 2,000, or the cap of §1.
  - A run that cannot be completed leaves its arm with fewer runs. The rows read

  - A row that reads an arm with fewer than two runs has no value.

## 2. The choices that have to be made at signature time

### 2.1 The text the first call carries — Sergi's choice; the drafter recommends T1

Both candidates replace v2b's `BLIND_BASE` in v1d's first call, and leave its
first sentence as it is. Neither names a queue or a rule.

- **T1, the region asked for.**

  > BASE DE REGLAS: no se te muestra en este paso. Escribe tu regla para este
  > ticket con la region entera de la regla de la lista que lo gobierna: la
  > primera de la lista cuyas condiciones casa el ticket. No le anadas

  > la lista: una regla dentro de otra solo le gana a esa otra, y el resto de la
  > region que debia gobernar se queda con reglas de otras colas.

**Why the drafter recommends T1.** It is the text the readings propose, and it
separates the two things that can still fail. If v1d's drafts hold their part
(`WR-a`) and the queue still is not compiled, the loss is the round's or the
engine's. Under T2 a refutation of `WR-b` could be the drafting again, and the
plan would not say which. **What T1 costs** is the proposer's own drafting at the
first call: under T1 the first call is close to the transcriber's, and no row
reads what the model drafts unasked. T2 reads that, and is the next plan if T1's
rows hold.

### 2.2 The round is v1c's, unchanged — the drafter recommends it

v1c refuses an answer that leaves a listed rule without a side, and a whole draft
meets longer lists. A round that accepted a partial placement, and left the
unplaced pairs to conflict, would take that risk away. It would also be a second
change, and no row could then say which change did what. `WR-c` reads the risk.
If it is refuted, the partial round is the next plan, as `IDEAS.md` carries it
under *What `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md` opens*.

### 2.3 Three runs an arm, the arms alternating, and the cap — the drafter recommends it

arm. Run `k` of both arms reads `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s draws of `c r{k}`. The cap is
400 escalations a run. Both are `PLAN_PLACEMENT.md`'s.

It reads `PLAN_CHANNEL.md`'s smoke draws. It checks the pin, the text and the

### 2.6 The pin is DeepInfra's fp8 endpoint, with no bridge — by the rule agreed on 2026-11-06
